How stat points work

  • Health: +20 maximum HP per point.
  • Damage: +4 attack per point.
  • Speed: +3% initiative per point.
  • Critical: +2% critical chance per point, capped at 60%.
  • Defence: +10 defence per point; damage reduction uses 10% of defence.
  • Regeneration: +3 HP each turn, capped at maximum health.
